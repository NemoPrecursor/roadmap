How to use this page

Every week has three parts. The grey paragraph explains in plain English what the topic actually is and why it matters — read that first, before you watch anything. The checkboxes are the work. The pill links go straight to the exact free video, lab or download you need.

You are not expected to understand any of this yet. That is the entire point of the plan. Week 1 assumes you know nothing, and each week only depends on the ones before it.

Two rules that matter more than the schedule: never skip the lab — watching a video teaches you the words, doing the lab teaches you the thing — and if you fall behind, move the exam date, don't skip weeks. There is a glossary at the bottom for any term that ambushes you.

Your progress lives in this page's web address. Tick a box and the address updates itself. Bookmark it, or copy the link and mail it to yourself, and your ticks travel to any device.
