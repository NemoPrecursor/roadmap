How to use this page

Read the green box, do that one thing, tick it. That is the whole system. You never need to plan or decide anything — the page always tells you the single next action and how long it should take.

It opens on this week only, because 70 weeks at once is not useful. Tap any other week to peek at it, or switch to Whole plan if you want the map.

Two rules worth more than the schedule. Never skip a hands-on task — watching teaches you the words, doing teaches you the thing. And if you fall behind, move the exam date rather than skipping weeks.

Anything underlined like this is a glossary term: hover it, or tap it on a phone.
